1. IMPORTANT INFORMATION ABOUT DATA RELATIONSHIPS

Our Role in Data Processing

For Restaurant Clients: We act as a Data Controller for information you provide to us directly when creating accounts, subscribing to services, and communicating with us.

For End User Data: We act as a Data Processor on behalf of our Restaurant Clients. The Restaurant is the Data Controller for their customers' data. We process this data solely according to the Restaurant's instructions and our Data Processing Agreement.

Important Notice for End Users: If you are placing orders through a restaurant that uses DELEVER:

  • Your contractual relationship is with the restaurant, not DELEVER
  • The restaurant is responsible for your data and must provide you with their own privacy notice
  • We process your data only as a technical service provider to the restaurant
  • For questions about your data, please contact the restaurant directly

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

2.1 Restaurant Client Information

Information You Provide:

  • Account Information: Business name, contact person name, email address, phone number, business address
  • Business Documentation: Trade license, VAT registration, business verification documents
  • Payment Information: Billing address, tax identification numbers, payment method details (processed through third-party payment processors)
  • User Credentials: Usernames, passwords, API keys
  • Communications: Support requests, feedback, correspondence
  • Platform Usage: Menu items, pricing, restaurant settings, delivery zones, operating hours

Information We Collect Automatically:

  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, operating system
  • Usage Data: Feature usage, login times, page views, system performance metrics
  • Location Data: Approximate location based on IP address
  • Cookies and Tracking: See Section 9 for details

2.2 End User Information (Processed on Behalf of Restaurants)

When you place orders through restaurants using our Platform, we process:

  • Order Information: Name, delivery address, phone number, email address
  • Transaction Data: Order details, payment information, delivery instructions
  • Communication Data: Order-related messages, delivery updates
  • Device Information: IP address, device type for fraud prevention

Important: We process this data solely as a processor for the restaurant. We do not use End User data for our own marketing or sell it to third parties.

2.3 Website Visitor Information

  • Contact Forms: Name, email, phone number, company name, inquiry details
  • Newsletter Signups: Email address, name (optional)
  • Cookies and Analytics: See Section 9

META PLATFORM DATA

Where a Restaurant Client connects its own Facebook Page or Instagram business account to our customer-communication tools, we receive data from Meta Platforms, Inc. via the Meta Graph API strictly on that client's behalf: page and Instagram account identifiers and access tokens, messages sent to the connected accounts, comments left under their posts, and lead form submissions (name, phone number and answers provided by the person submitting the form).

We use this data solely to display those conversations, comments and leads to the connected business and to deliver its replies. We do not sell Platform Data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with third parties except the service providers listed below acting on our instructions. Platform Data is retained while the page connection is active and deleted upon disconnection or on request (see our Data Deletion Instructions).

SERVICE PROVIDERS (DATA PROCESSORS)

  • Neon Inc. (USA, Germany) — managed database
  • OpenAI LLC (USA) — AI-assisted processing of message texts
  • Together Computer Inc. (USA) — text embeddings for internal search

6. DATA SECURITY

6.1 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption at rest and in transit (TLS/SSL)
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular security assessments and penetration testing
  • Incident response procedures
  • Employee training and confidentiality agreements
  • PCI DSS compliance for payment data
  • ISO 27001 aligned practices

6.2 Data Breach Response

In the event of a data breach:

  • We will notify affected Restaurant Clients within 72 hours
  • We will assist with End User notifications as required
  • We will document and investigate the incident
  • We will implement measures to prevent recurrence

6.3 Your Security Responsibilities

Restaurant Clients must:

  • Maintain secure passwords
  • Restrict access to authorized personnel
  • Report security incidents promptly
  • Ensure their own compliance with data protection laws
