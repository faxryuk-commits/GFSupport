Data Deletion Instructions

Delever · GFSupport customer communication platform

This page explains how to have your personal data deleted from Delever's systems, including any data we received from Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram) — such as your messages to a connected business account, comments, or lead form submissions.

If you contacted a restaurant or business using Delever

  1. Send an email to fyusupov@delever.uz with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Tell us how you contacted the business (Instagram Direct, Facebook Messenger, comment, or lead form) and the account name or phone number you used, so we can locate your records.
  3. We will delete your messages, comments, lead submissions and contact details from our systems within 30 days and confirm by reply.

If you are a business that connected a Facebook Page or Instagram account

Disconnect the account in Настройки → Интеграции → Instagram и Facebook. Disconnection unsubscribes our application from your Page and revokes the stored access token. To also remove historical conversations, comments and leads, email the address above from the account owner's address — we delete them within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Message texts and attachments metadata, comment texts, lead form answers, sender identifiers received from Meta, and access tokens. Aggregated statistics that contain no personal data may be retained.
